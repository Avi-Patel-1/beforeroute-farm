# p114b56 rationale

At T0 the worst setup endpoints are outputs: G10 (0.833 to 0.895 ns), G18 (0.838), G09 (0.967), G20 (1.069). All launch from one 4-bit load-enable register (i14705, i22835, i9097, i25353) at x 167-196, y 177-184 um. Output slack shrinks at 0.8 of the period and carries the full launch clock latency, so these paths likely stay critical as the clock tightens. Pins only change their wire. The east edge at y about 170-190 is the closest edge point to the register and its decode logic (x 200-290).

- **G20 E2** (first): keeps G20 on its current slot (179.86 um). It only guards this path against a worse stock pin in rebuilds. Expected change about 0; confidence 0.6.
- **G18 E2** (174.4 um, was N at x=216): the a22o_4 i8237 to a21o_4 i24017 to G18 path now runs about 114 um north. E2 cuts about 40 um from the register path; data-register paths stay about the same. Expect G18 0.03-0.08 ns earlier; confidence 0.6.
- **G10 E2** (169-187 um, was 6 bits on E at 201-275 and 2 on N): register-to-pin distance for bits 0, 4, 6 and 7 falls from about 235 to about 150 um, so expect them 0.03-0.08 ns faster. Bits 1, 2, 3 and 5 move by 10 um or less, so the worst bit, G10[2], is roughly unchanged and T0 WNS may not move. The G14 input registers feeding each bit sit at y 174-196, beside the new pins. Other data paths into G10 get up to 30-80 um longer but have about 1.7 ns of slack. Confidence 0.55.

Not moved: G09 (the west edge is as close as any other), inputs (at least 1.9 ns of slack), constant or unconnected groups.
