# p00d863 rationale: ABSTAIN

**What limits the clock.** About ten paths sit within 0.06 ns of each other at 4.6 ns (0.505 to 0.56 ns slack): G04 to i1450 (through a 1.1 ns hold-delay gate), i372 to the top-left accumulator registers, G02, RST to i1450, G05 to i4246, and G04 to i2065/i1907.

They share one cause. The control cluster (G04, G05, i372, G02/G07 drivers, anchored on S at x 91 to 100) gates all 110 flip-flops. Register slack falls about 5.5 ps per micrometre of distance from it (r = -0.73). With distance removed, the groups look alike.

**Why no move.**
- Control pins: S at x 91 to 100 is already near the best edge spot; the farthest registers are about 170 um from any edge point.
- G06 to W2: top accumulator bits gain about 0.1 ns, but bit 0 (i4246) moves about 75 um from the cluster, losing about 0.4 ns. S1 has the same problem.
- G06 to S2: clears the accumulator paths, but the next limit (i2065/i1907, 0.548) is only 0.025 ns better, and it crowds i1450 and moves the accumulator far from RST.
- G03 to E2 or E1: not among the limiting paths. E2 hurts G03[3] (about 0.57 ns); E1 moves outputs farther from RST.

Every option trades one path family for another, and the stock pins already match the stock placement. Expected gain is at most about zero, with real downside.

**Confidence that abstaining is no worse than any single move: 0.6.**
