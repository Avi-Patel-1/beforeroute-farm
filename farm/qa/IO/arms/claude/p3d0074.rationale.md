# p3d0074 rationale: ABSTAIN

**What limits the clock at T0 (7.02 ns):** worst slack 0.718 ns on output G02[5]. The other G02 bits sit at 0.78-1.06 ns, the worst reg-to-reg path at 0.894 ns, and G07 at 0.92 ns. Output and input slack shrinks by 0.8 x dT, so G02, G07 and G05 stay the most critical I/O paths as the clock tightens.

**Why no move:**
- **G02 (8-bit output).** Each bit is a mux. Select flops near the die centre feed the first level at y=147-153. Data flops at y=168-242 then pull the last levels north-east. The tool placed each bit by itself: 3 on N3 and 5 on the east edge at y=174-222. A moved group must sit in one contiguous run at a segment centre: E2 lands at y=129-145, E3 at about 225, N3 at x of about 215. A soft-anchored quadratic placement of the whole netlist (critical nets weighted x3) shows each option lengthening some bits' critical paths. G02[5], the worst path, gets 15-36 um longer under E2 and 25-30 um longer under E3 or N3, an estimated -0.02 to -0.04 ns at T0. Only near-zero anchoring (unrealistic cell collapse) made E2 look better.
- **G07, G05, G13, G11, G06, G16.** Their last or first logic sits near the current pin. Snapping them to a segment centre was neutral or worse by 2-35 um in every model.
- **Other groups.** They have 2.6-5.2 ns of slack, and pins cannot affect internal reg-to-reg paths. Moving them only adds placement noise.

**Expected effect:** none. The clock should match the tool's pins (claim 6.302, first rebuild at 6.35).

**Confidence that abstaining is at least as good as any single listed move: 0.6.**
